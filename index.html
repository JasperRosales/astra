About Astra

Astra is a private journal where every entry becomes a star. Its mood sets the color, how much it mattered sets the brightness, and entries that share a chapter of your life join into a constellation.

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, drag a star to move it. Use the timeline to see your sky on any past day, or press ▶ to watch it form.

Everything is stored in your browser (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the Canvas API. Developed with the help of an AI coding assistant (Claude Code). Fonts: Cormorant Garamond & DM Mono (Google Fonts).
